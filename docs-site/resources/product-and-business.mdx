## Product layers

| Layer | What it is | Status | Price hypothesis |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Scan** | `halflife scan`, the CI gate, registry reads, the open spec | Built | **Free, forever.** A security signal nobody can read isn't a signal. |
| **Monitor** | Continuous re-checking of a team's circuits, alerts, auto-invalidation | Not built | Paid, per circuit per month. **Unpriced.** |
| **Feed** | Machine-readable list of which deployed systems are currently unsafe | Not built | Paid, for exchanges, custodians, and insurers. **Unpriced.** |

No prices are stated. Nobody has been asked what they'd pay, and an invented number would be exactly the unmeasured claim the project exists to avoid.

## Landscape

| Category | Examples | Where they stop |
|---|---|---|
| Rust dependency scanners | `cargo audit`, `cargo-deny`, Dependabot, `osv-scanner` | Report on crates, not circuits. No machine-readable claim a program can consume. |
| Commercial supply-chain security | Snyk, Socket | General appsec. No on-chain surface. |
| ZK audit firms | Trail of Bits, Veridise, Zellic, zkSecurity, OpenZeppelin | A snapshot with no enforced expiry. **Natural partners**: an audit could be what a higher-tier passport attests. |
| ZK bug catalogues | 0xPARC zk-bug-tracker, zkSecurity zkBugs | Education, not a live exposure feed |
| On-chain monitoring and response | Hypernative, Forta, OpenZeppelin Defender | Watch chain behaviour. A counterfeit ZK proof looks valid. **Complementary**: their pause button can consume Halflife's signal. |
| Static and formal circuit tools | Picus, ZK Vanguard, Circomspect | "Is my circuit wrong?" rather than "did a library I trust just become wrong?" |

### Where Halflife is different

1. **It joins things that are normally separate:** advisory data, then the dependency graph, then a signed claim, then an on-chain halt.
2. **The unit is the circuit**, not the crate.
3. **It fails safe by construction.** Monitoring tools fail open.
4. **It's honest about depth**, with C0–C5 tiers and only C1 claimed.
5. **The free layer is the wedge**: a CI gate that's useful to one team on day one.

### Where it's weaker

No customers, brand, sales team, or audited code. Incumbents could add an advisory-aware feature. **No moat is claimed.** The defence is an open, frozen spec and a head start on the on-chain half.

| Measure | Today |

| Maintainers of affected crates notified | 3 of 4 (11 Oct 2026) |

**Kill criteria:** if after ten genuine conversations no team would put the gate in CI or pay for monitoring, Monitor and Feed don't get built, and the project remains a free public tool plus the spec.

## Risks

- Value concentrates in incidents, which are rare.
- Halting a protocol is a hard ask. CI and monitoring are the realistic first adopters.
- There's no engine above C1, and automated soundness discovery is an open research problem.
- A stolen issuer key can grief. That's bounded by quorum.
- The on-chain program is unaudited and single-keyed.
- Solo founder.
