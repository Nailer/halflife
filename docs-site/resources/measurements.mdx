## Compute cost on Solana

| Operation | Compute units | Reproduce |
|---|---|---|
| **Consumer passport check** | **1,520 CU** | `cargo run --release -p halflife-bench` |
| Publish a signed passport | 11,550 CU | same |
| Register an issuer | 7,406 CU | same |

1,520 CU is **0.76%** of a default 200,000 CU transaction budget and **0.0015%** of the 100M CU block limit.

**What's included:** account deserialization and instruction dispatch, taken from the runtime's own `consumed N of M compute units` log. Bracketing only the comparisons would give a smaller number nobody could reproduce in practice.

**Environment:** LiteSVM 0.6 against the SBF artifacts from `anchor build`.

## Containment time on devnet

| Scenario | Containment | On-chain writes | Record |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dependency compromise | **4 s** | Invalidation published | `exercises/dependency-compromise-1790859361.json` |
| Relayer censorship | **41 s** | **Exactly 1** (the baseline). Nothing published. | `exercises/relayer-censorship-1790859373.json` |

The censorship figure is bounded by the drill's **45 s TTL**. Containment under censorship is at most your TTL plus one slot. Reproduce with `halflife-exercise run …` and re-check with `halflife-exercise verify …`.
