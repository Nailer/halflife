Progress is tracked **in the repository** (`docs/build-order.md`), so tick boxes move in the same commit as the work. Gates are stopping points: work halts at one until a decision is made.

<Accordion title="In plain terms" icon="lightbulb">
This is the project plan: what's finished, what's next, and the checkpoints where the project deliberately stops to decide whether to continue.
</Accordion>

## Proof boundary: done

<Steps>
  <Step title="Stage 0 · Disclosure first" icon="check">
    B0.1 disclosure policy before the first scan. B0.2 embargo limits written down. **Gate A approved.**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stage 1 · Freeze the protocol (v1.0.0-format)" icon="check">
    125-byte core, signed sequence, supersession and replay rules, disclosure off the core, forward-only revocation, clock bounds, absence-is-not-permission, conformance vectors plus an independent implementation. **Gate B frozen.** The vectors caught the JSON-number defect first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stage 2 · Registry" icon="check">
    SQLite store, `register`/`list`/`deps`, reverse index, a real fleet of 8 systems. **Gate C confirmed.**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stage 3 · Projection" icon="check">
    Pure projection crate, disclosure-aware closure, `impact`, leak tests. **Gate D** waived, evidence reported.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stage 4 · Solana enforcement" icon="check">
    Anchor scaffold, publish/supersede/revoke with ed25519, reference consumer, CU benchmark, devnet deployment. **Gate E** (does the number support the thesis?): 1,520 CU.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stage 5 · Propagation" icon="check">
    EVM destination, origin dispatch, Aggregation ISM, destination expiry and high-water mark. **Gate F:** kill the relayer and the consumer still blocks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stage 6 · Fire drill" icon="check">
    Event ledger, both scenarios, third-party `exercise verify`. **Gate G:** measured, not simulated.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The three hard gates

| Gate | Blocks | Cost of skipping |
|---|---|---|
| **A** Disclosure policy | Registering any third party | A finding about someone else's project with no agreed process |
| **B** Format freeze | Solana program, EVM contract, SDK | A migration across four runtimes and every published passport |
| **E** CU benchmark | The Solana thesis itself | Propagation built for enforcement nobody can afford to call |
